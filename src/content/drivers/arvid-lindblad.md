## Why he matters

Lindblad is the most-hyped Red Bull junior since Max Verstappen. Helmut Marko, Red Bull's driver advisor, fast-tracked him through F4, F3, and F2. Lindblad won the latter in 2025, his first season at the level. The Racing Bulls seat is a one-year audition; if he impresses, he's the obvious candidate to replace whoever doesn't make it at the senior team.

## How he drives

Quick everywhere, but the calling card is qualifying. His F2 pole laps in 2025 had a smoothness usually reserved for established F1 drivers. The open question (same as for any rookie) is how he handles a long F1 race after a tough Saturday.

## Watch for

- His head-to-head qualifying record against teammate Liam Lawson. That's the only metric Red Bull will look at.
- His Friday practice sessions. Lindblad is one of the few rookies who can be expected to deliver clean long-runs immediately.
- His radio voice: calmly, almost flatly chatty for a rookie, similar to Antonelli.
