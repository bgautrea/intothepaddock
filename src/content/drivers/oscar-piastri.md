## Why he matters

Piastri arrived at McLaren in 2023 paired with Norris, two years his senior, and within a season was equal-fast on race days and sometimes faster in qualifying. He was second in the 2025 championship, losing the title to Norris by 12 points in Abu Dhabi after a strategy mix-up in the final stint.

## How he drives

Smooth, surgical, almost emotionless on team radio. He brakes a fraction earlier than Norris and uses less curb on corner entry, which sometimes costs him in qualifying but pays back in tyre life and consistency on Sundays.

## Watch for

- His race starts and first laps: among the best on the grid.
- His radio when angry. Rare, but worth listening for; he sounds genuinely surprised every time.
- His qualifying lap when conditions are at the limit (rain, big track-temperature swings). He often beats Norris in those sessions.
