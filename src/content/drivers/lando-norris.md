## Why he matters

Norris was McLaren's first world champion since Hamilton in 2008 and the team's first since Mika Häkkinen if you discount Hamilton's McLaren title (which was Mercedes-engined). The 2025 championship fight went down to the final laps in Abu Dhabi between Norris, his teammate Oscar Piastri, and Max Verstappen — the closest three-way title finish since 2010.

## How he drives

He drives a car visually softer than Verstappen — fewer steering corrections, smoother on the throttle out of slow corners — but is just as quick. His weaknesses, when he has them, are starts and the occasional tyre-temperature struggle in qualifying when track conditions change between Q2 and Q3.

## Watch for

- His qualifying laps in the second half of the season — he was on pole eight times in 2025.
- His relationship with teammate Oscar Piastri — the polite intra-team rivalry of 2024 became less polite by the end of 2025.
- His karting-buddy friendship with Verstappen, which has noticeably cooled since they started fighting for the championship.
