## Why he matters

Sainz is the experienced, methodical, multi-team driver every team-in-rebuild wants. He spent four years at Ferrari, winning in Britain, Singapore, Australia, and Mexico — and was replaced when Hamilton signed. He turned down Audi and chose Williams under James Vowles, betting on the rebuild rather than the resources.

## How he drives

Sainz is the modern definition of a complete driver. He's not the fastest qualifier in the field, but he's a 9/10 across qualifying, race pace, tyre management, wheel-to-wheel racing, and engineering feedback. Teams who hire Sainz hire a rolling development engineer.

## Watch for

- His engineer-radio communication — Sainz delivers the cleanest, most useful information of any driver in the paddock; engineers love him.
- His Q3 laps when the car is unexpectedly competitive — he punches above weight on the surprise upgrade weeks.
- His relationship with teammate Alex Albon — both are Williams's heart, and they're teaching each other.
