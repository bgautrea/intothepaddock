A modern Formula 1 car is, depending on who you ask, either a 750-kilogram missile or a 17-million-dollar science experiment. It's the most sophisticated four-wheeled machine on Earth — and like any complex tool, it's easier to understand once you break it down into its parts.

This is a placeholder version of the page that exists to validate the build pipeline. The full interactive cutaway and 12+ part deep-dives ship in Milestone 4.

<AnalogyCallout>
  Reading this page in order is like reading a recipe — each part builds on the one before it.
  Start at the chassis (the "skeleton") and work outward.
</AnalogyCallout>

<div style="display: grid; grid-template-columns: repeat(auto-fit, minmax(280px, 1fr)); gap: 1.5rem; margin-block: 3rem;">

<StepCard number={1} title="Chassis & Survival Cell" accent="var(--kerb-yellow)" analogy="It's the rollcage of a stunt car, but built from material that's stiffer than steel and lighter than aluminum.">
The carbon-fiber tub at the heart of the car. The driver sits inside this monocoque. It has to survive impacts that would crumple anything else.
</StepCard>

<StepCard number={2} title="Power Unit" accent="#dc0000" analogy="A hybrid V6 turbo with the energy recovery of a Prius — but tuned to produce ~1,000 horsepower.">
A 1.6-litre turbocharged V6 paired with two electric motors (MGU-K and MGU-H) and a battery pack. Burns fuel at over 50% thermal efficiency, which is unheard of in road cars.
</StepCard>

<StepCard number={3} title="Aerodynamics" accent="#27f4d2" analogy="An upside-down airplane. Wings push the car onto the road instead of into the sky.">
Front wing, rear wing, floor, sidepods, and diffuser all work together to generate downforce — the invisible force that lets these cars corner at 5g.
</StepCard>

<StepCard number={4} title="Tyres" accent="#fcd700" analogy="A Pirelli soft compound has a useful life of roughly 20 minutes before it stops working.">
Five compounds, supplied exclusively by Pirelli. Choosing which to run, when to change them, and how to manage their temperature is most of race strategy.
</StepCard>
