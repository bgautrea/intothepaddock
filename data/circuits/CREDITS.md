# Circuit GeoJSON Credits

These track outline GeoJSON files are vendored from an upstream open-source
project and used unmodified.

- **Source:** https://github.com/bacinger/f1-circuits
- **License:** MIT

## Notes

- The files in this directory are **unmodified copies** of the upstream
  GeoJSON files; only their filenames have been changed to match our
  internal track slugs (e.g. `au-1953.geojson` → `melbourne.geojson`).
- Redistribution is permitted under the terms of the upstream MIT license.
- Madrid (the new-for-2026 Madring street circuit) is intentionally **not**
  included here, as it does not yet exist in the upstream repository.
